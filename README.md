## 🕹️ About

Another Eden Begins is a single-player, time-traveling JRPG developed by STUDIO PRISMA and published by Wright Flyer Studios. Set across antiquity, the present, and the future, it follows Aldo as he journeys beyond time and space to rescue his sister Feinne and protect a lost future. Players build parties of 19 characters, explore story-driven regions, complete character and encounter quests, and fight through command-based battles with strategic party synergy and the powerful Another Force ability. After completing the main story, New Game+ introduces new story paths and more than 10 endings shaped by player choices.

Another Eden Begins is made for players who enjoy story-rich JRPGs, turn-based strategy, time-travel narratives, and replayable single-player adventures.

## ✨ Features

- 🔥 **Another Force** — Charge the Another Force gauge to unleash a devastating burst of attacks.
- ⏳ **Time-Traveling Adventure** — Explore antiquity, the present, and the future across one connected story.
- 👥 **Character-Driven Party** — Recruit and develop a cast of 19 characters with individual stories and abilities.
- ⚔️ **Command-Based Battles** — Build effective party combinations and exploit enemy weaknesses through strategic commands.
- 🎼 **Cinematic Soundtrack** — Experience a fully voiced main story supported by music from acclaimed JRPG composers.
- 🎭 **Branching New Game+** — Replay the adventure with upgraded characters and discover more than 10 different endings.
